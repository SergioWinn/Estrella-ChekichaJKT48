# JKT48 Chekicha Archive

An archive for browsing event history, discovering member appearances, and managing a personal chekicha collection. Audience: JKT48 fans and collectors. All primary surfaces are Operate mode.

- Timeline at `/` (`/timeline` alias): events grouped by month; event category, series, team, member and text filters.
- Birthday and Graduation have one member; Roulette can have Slot A only or Slots A and B. Unassigned slots remain visible; nonexistent slots do not.
- Members: alphabetical directory, member history, show/event ranking and comparison. Ranking excludes Birthday/Graduation; preserve accessible pause and reduced-motion behavior.
- My collection: authenticated, grouped by member, quantities managed per event slot. Adding a duplicate slot increases quantity; managing replaces quantity.
- Admin: role-protected event, member and assignment management. Never bypass authentication for previews.
- Keep real stored photos/artwork and factual event data. Mockup assignments and generated artwork are not production data.
- Current scope: approved Collector's Album layout with the Chekicha icon-inspired palette. No data migration.
